Filaments:

I use PLA filaments to do all my prints. PLA is a a great all-around filament to use and is perfect for the types of prints I do. PLA doesn't require very high heat. PLA also has a wide range of colors and finishes which is perfect for decorative prints. It's also low cost and easy to work with. Another bonus: It's biodegradable!
