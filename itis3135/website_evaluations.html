Website Evaluations

Amazon

  • Contrast: Amazon's website has great contrast in regards to their space for their showcased categories.
  • Repetition: Amazon's website does very well at keeping the same colors throughout the website. It also does well at keeping the same layout for the listings of items.
  • Alignment: Amazon's website has great alignment and keeps everything in a layout to where it's easy to scroll through items seamlessly.
  • Proximity: Amazon's website lays out their items in a way where you can see the image of the item and all its information close together. There is also good use of white space to ensure there is enough room for long item names.
