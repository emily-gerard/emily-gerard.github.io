Website Evaluations

Amazon

  • Contrast: Amazon's website has great contrast in regards to their space for their showcased categories.
  • Repetition: Amazon's website does very well at keeping the same colors throughout the website. It also does well at keeping the same layout for the listings of items.
  • Alignment: Amazon's website has great alignment and keeps everything in a layout to where it's easy to scroll through items seamlessly.
  • Proximity: Amazon's website lays out their items in a way where you can see the image of the item and all its information close together. There is also good use of white space to ensure there is enough room for long item names.

Arngren

  • Contrast: Arngren's website does a very poor job of contrast through very similar font sizes, category sizes, and colors. The font sizes are all very similar and the colors don't help draw emphasis to anything.
  • Repetition: Arngren's website does a horrible job at repetition. While there is repetition, it's not streamlined. The category names all have differring colors that clutters the page. The item and category borders are all different in shape, color, and size.
  • Alignment: There is no alignment whatsoever on Arngren's website. Category names extend into pictures, the item images are all over the page, and the names of items aren't aligned with the images.
  • Proximity: The elements on this website have horrible proximity to eachother. There are many overlapping titles and images. The names of items are too close to one another and other items which makes it difficult to tell what each item is called.
